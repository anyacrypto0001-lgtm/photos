Your fonts

If you delete a font, anything that used it switches to a plain font.

Help
Using a Canon EOS R100 or other mirrorless/DSLR camera

Connect it either way, then choose it in the Camera list above:

USB: install Canon's free EOS Webcam Utility, quit Canon EOS Utility if it is running, turn the camera's Wi-Fi off and set the dial to movie mode. Choose "EOS Webcam Utility" in the list.

HDMI: a capture card (for example Elgato Cam Link) from the camera's HDMI port. Turn on clean HDMI output in the camera menu. Choose the capture card in the list.

Also set the camera's auto power-off to Disable and use a dummy battery (DC coupler) for long events. If the camera is mounted on its side, set "Camera mounted" above.

Filters

Tap a filter to try it on the live camera below. Take a test photo shows what a real photo looks like with it (nothing is saved). Make your own with sliders, or upload a colour LUT (.cube) from Lightroom, Photoshop or a filter pack.

Modes guests can pick
Timing and sound

Shows the booth's camera screen and runs the real countdown, flash and sound, without taking a photo. Settings reopen afterwards.

Photo extras

For photo sessions (strip, grid, single). Guests get them alongside the print, on screen and through the QR code.

Settings for each mode appear here once you tick it above.

Scribble mode

Guests draw on a glass panel between them and the camera while a video records, then pose for a final photo in its own design (Edit print templates → Scribble).

Printing

Photo strip, grid, single and scribble photos print by themselves as the results screen opens. GIFs and boomerangs aren't printed.

Looking for printers…

Add more than one printer to share the work: each print goes to the working printer that handles its size, preferring the one with the fewest waiting jobs. DNP printers (DS-RX1HS and others): choose the finish, quality and sizes for each one here. 2×6 strips use the printer's own 2-inch cut, so you no longer need a second Windows printer.

Printer drivers

DNP drivers are bundled in the drivers folder next to LePetique.exe. Install asks Windows for permission and installs the driver; then switch the printer on and plug it in, and Windows adds it. To add another driver, put its folder (the one with the .inf file) inside drivers and tap Refresh printers.

Guest downloads (QR code)

Each session uploads to your free Supabase project, and guests can download it any time, even after the event. The booth needs internet; if it drops, photos wait and upload when it's back. Setup steps are in the README.

Upload the guest-viewer\index.html file from the Le Petique folder to a free host once (steps in the README), then paste its address here. Leave it empty and the QR opens just the main picture.
