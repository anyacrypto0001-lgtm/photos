Capture

Canon camera settings

Using a Canon EOS R100 or other mirrorless/DSLR camera

Connect it either way, then choose it in the Camera list above:

USB: install Canon's free EOS Webcam Utility, quit Canon EOS Utility if it is running, turn the camera's Wi-Fi off and set the dial to movie mode. Choose "EOS Webcam Utility" in the list.

HDMI: a capture card (for example Elgato Cam Link) from the camera's HDMI port. Turn on clean HDMI output in the camera menu. Choose the capture card in the list.

Also set the camera's auto power-off to Disable and use a dummy battery (DC coupler) for long events. If the camera is mounted on its side, set "Camera mounted" above.

Scribble mode (turn it on under Modes). Guests draw on a glass panel between them and the camera while a video records, then pose for a final photo in your Single design.

Extras for photo sessions (strip, grid, single). Guests get them alongside the print, on screen and through the QR code.

Where photos are saved on this laptop

Guest downloads (QR code)

Each session uploads to your free Supabase project, and guests can download it any time, even after the event. The booth needs internet; if it drops, photos wait and upload when it's back. Setup steps are in the README.

Upload the guest-viewer\index.html file from the Le Petique folder to a free host once (steps in the README), then paste its address here. Leave it empty and the QR opens just the main picture.

Booth window

Takes effect next time you open LePetique.exe. F11 switches full screen on and off any time.

A backup file holds every setting, design, booth screen and picture (not the photo gallery). Use it to move the booth to another laptop or keep a copy.
